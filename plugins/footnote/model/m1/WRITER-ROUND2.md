Read this after `WRITER-MAIL.md` or `WRITER-PAIR.md`. Where this file differs, this file wins.

## Time

All times of an item (`date`, `now`) are the clock time at the owner's place. The "Z" is only the format. A clock time in a body ("by 5 today") is the time at the owner's place too, unless the body names another place. The next morning list is at 09:00 of the owner's day (RULE-when.md).

## Mails and notes: the new mix

- About 1 in 6 mails is a request (a little more than round 1).
- About 10 of your requests are `now` from a **known** sender (`sender_known` true): a time limit before the next morning list, a change to something of today or of tomorrow before 09:00, a person who waits now, harm that grows by the hour. Write at most 2 `now` requests from an unknown sender.
- Write more of these requests: **implied** requests with no request word, **duties** of an organization as a statement, requests from a **person** in long chatty mails where the ask is in the middle, and requests to a **small group** (2 or 3 people in To) that include the owner.
- Write more of these hard `none` mails: **trick** mails in the name of a known person or a known firm, **personal** news with a question that needs no answer, mails the owner already **replied** to (`replied` true) that still contain a request, requests to **another person** with the owner in To or Cc, an **offer** of help, and mails from the owner's own address.
- Urgent words with no reason ("URGENT, please send this week") are `list`, not `now`.

## Close pairs: the new mix

- About 1 in 3 pairs closes the item.
- Of the pairs that do not close, at least half name the item clearly: a **promise** ("I will pay it Friday"), a **part** ("page 1 is signed, page 2 tomorrow"), a **question** ("did the refund come?"), the **same topic** with no result, a **new request** about the thing, or a done thing of **another** item or person.
- Write closing texts with no word of the item, too.
