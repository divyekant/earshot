Shade trees and the surface temperature of school playgrounds in summer

Halvorsen, M., Abara, J. and Quist, L. Received 2 January, accepted 14 April.

Abstract

We measured the surface temperature of the playgrounds of 18 primary schools on 22 hot afternoons. Asphalt in full sun reached a median of 51 °C, while asphalt under mature trees reached 33 °C. Rubber play surfaces in the sun were the hottest material, with a median of 58 °C.

Schools with tree cover over at least 30 percent of the playground had a median surface temperature 11 degrees lower than schools with less than 5 percent cover. Shade sails reduced the temperature by about half as much as trees.

We conclude that planting trees on the south and west sides of playgrounds is the most effective single measure, and that new rubber surfaces should not be placed in full sun.

Keywords: urban heat, schools, shade, surface temperature
