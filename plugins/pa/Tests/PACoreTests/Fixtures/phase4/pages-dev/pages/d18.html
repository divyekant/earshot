Clatterbridge United 2, Harwood Rovers 2: late penalty saves a point

Match report by Imogen Barr, at Foundry Lane. Saturday 28 September.

A penalty in the 93rd minute from captain Lewis Achterberg gave Clatterbridge United a 2-2 draw against Harwood Rovers, after the home side had been two goals down at half time.

Harwood scored twice in the first 20 minutes. Femi Adeyinka headed in a corner after eight minutes, and Rory Gault added a second from outside the box after a mistake in the home defence.

United improved after the break. Substitute Kai Moreno pulled one goal back in the 61st minute with a low shot. In added time, Harwood's goalkeeper brought down Moreno in the box, and Achterberg scored the penalty to the left.

The draw keeps United in ninth place in the table, with 14 points from 10 games. They play away at Steeple Norton on Tuesday.

The attendance was 3,412.
