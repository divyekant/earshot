We tested 6 cordless vacuum cleaners: the Tervo V9 wins

By the Fair Test lab team · Tested in August

We tested six cordless stick vacuums from £149 to £499 in our lab and in three real homes. Each one cleaned the same carpet, hard floor and stairs, and we measured the run time from a full battery.

The Tervo V9 picked up the most dust on carpet and ran for 48 minutes in normal mode. At £279 it is not the cheapest, but it beat two models that cost more than £400.

The cheapest model, the Brightwell Swift, is a poor buy: its battery lasted 24 minutes and it left visible dust on the carpet after three passes.

Two models were too heavy to use on stairs for long. If you have stairs, look for a model under 2.5 kg.
