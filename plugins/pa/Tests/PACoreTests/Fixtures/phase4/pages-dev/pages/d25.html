Flu vaccine clinics this autumn

The surgery runs walk-in flu vaccine clinics on three Saturdays: 28 September, 12 October and 26 October, from 9:00 to 12:30. You do not need an appointment.

The vaccine is free if you are 65 or older, pregnant, a carer, or if you have a long-term condition such as asthma, diabetes or heart disease. Children aged 2 and 3 get a nasal spray instead of an injection.

If you have a fever on the day, do not come. Book a later appointment instead.

Bring your NHS number if you know it. The whole visit takes about ten minutes, and we ask you to wait for five minutes after the vaccine.

If you cannot come on a Saturday, ask at reception for a weekday appointment with the practice nurse.
