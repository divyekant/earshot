Night market and lantern walk

On the last evening of the festival, the harbour wall turns into a night market with forty stalls of local food, crafts and books. At nine o'clock, a walk of paper lanterns leaves the market and follows the quay to the old lifeboat station.

When
Saturday 17 August, 18:00 to 22:30
Where
North harbour wall, Saltby
Price
Free entry. Lantern kits cost £4 at the welcome tent.

Make your own lantern

Children can make a lantern at the workshop tent from 18:00. Each kit has a willow frame, tissue paper and a battery light. The workshop closes at 20:30 so the glue can dry before the walk.

Access

The harbour wall is flat but has some uneven stones. A quiet area with seats is next to the harbour office. If it rains hard, the lantern walk moves to Sunday at the same time.
