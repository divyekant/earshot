Questions about the green bin collection

From 1 April the green bin is for garden waste only, and the service needs a yearly permit. These are the answers to the questions that people ask most often.

How much does the permit cost?

The permit costs £48 for one bin for twelve months. A second bin at the same address costs £30.

What can go in the green bin?

Grass, leaves, hedge cuttings, small branches and flowers. Do not put food, soil, stones or plastic bags in it.

How often is it collected?

Every two weeks from March to November, and once a month from December to February.

Can I share a bin with my neighbour?

Yes, if both households live on the same street. Only one permit is needed.

What if I move house?

Tell us your new address and we move the permit for free.
