Radiator cold at the top, warm at the bottom?
The radiator in our back bedroom is warm at the bottom but cold across the top 15 cm or so. All the other radiators are fine. The boiler is a combi, about six years old, and the pressure gauge shows 1.2 bar. What should I check first?
KieranGasSafe
That is almost always air at the top of the radiator. Turn the heating off and let the radiators cool. Put a cloth under the bleed valve at the top corner and open it a quarter turn with a radiator key. You will hear hissing. Close it when water comes out.
After that, check the pressure again. If it has dropped below 1 bar, top it up with the filling loop under the boiler until it reads between 1 and 1.5 bar.
Kieran – heating engineer, 20 years. Not a substitute for a visit.
tess_on_ivy_st
That worked. Lots of air came out and the radiator is hot all over now. Pressure went down to 0.8 bar, so I used the filling loop to bring it back to 1.3.
