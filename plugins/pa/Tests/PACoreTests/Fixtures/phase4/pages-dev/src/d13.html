Terms of hire

These terms apply from 1 September.

1. Membership

1.1 You must be a member to borrow tools. Membership costs £25 a year, or £10 a year for people on a low income.

1.2 Members must be 18 or older and live or work in the parish of Puddleby.

2. Borrowing

2.1 You can borrow up to three tools at a time, for up to seven days. You can extend a loan once by seven days if no other member has reserved the tool.

2.2 Power tools need a deposit of £20, which we return when the tool comes back clean and working.

3. Late returns and damage

3.1 A late return costs £1 for each day, up to the value of the tool.

3.2 Tell us at once if a tool breaks. We do not charge for normal wear. If a tool is damaged by misuse, we may ask you to pay for the repair.

4. Safety

4.1 You use the tools at your own risk. Read the safety card in each tool bag before use, and wear the protection it names.
