Apply for a resident parking permit

You can apply for a resident parking permit if you live in a controlled parking zone and your car is registered at your address.

A permit lets you park in the resident bays of your zone at any time. It does not guarantee a space.

Cost

  • First permit for a household: £85 a year
  • Second permit: £140 a year
  • Electric cars: £30 a year

What you need

Have these ready before you start:

  • the registration number of the car
  • a council tax bill or a tenancy agreement from the last 3 months
  • the logbook (V5C) in your name at this address

The council checks your documents within 5 working days. The permit is digital, so you do not get a paper copy for the windscreen.

If you move house, cancel the permit. The council refunds the unused full months.
