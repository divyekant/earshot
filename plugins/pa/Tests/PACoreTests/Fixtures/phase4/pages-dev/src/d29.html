How do I stop bread dough from sticking to the banneton?
14
My sourdough sticks to the cane banneton every time, and the loaf tears when I turn it out. I dust it with plain flour before I put the dough in. The dough is about 75 percent water and proves overnight in the fridge. What am I doing wrong?
Is the basket new? New baskets stick more. – miller_jo
2 answers
22 ✓
Use rice flour instead of wheat flour. Rice flour has no gluten, so it does not turn into glue when it gets wet overnight. Dust the basket well, and also dust the top of the dough before you put it in.
A new basket also helps after a few uses: let the flour build up a thin layer, and do not wash the basket with water. Brush it out and let it dry in a warm place.
Rice flour fixed it for me too. – crumbshot
5
At 75 percent water your dough is quite wet. Try a stronger shaping with more tension on the surface, or line the basket with a linen cloth dusted with flour.
