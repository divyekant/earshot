Bridge on Tanner Road to close for six weeks from 3 June

By Priya Lindqvist, transport reporter. Published 20 May, 07:15.

The road bridge over the Kestle on Tanner Road will close to all traffic from Monday 3 June to Friday 12 July. Engineers from the county found that two of the steel bearings under the deck have worn faster than expected, and they must be replaced before the winter.

About 9,000 vehicles cross the bridge each day. The official diversion goes through Pell Green and adds about 4 kilometres to a trip from the station to the north estate. People on foot and on bicycles can still cross on a temporary footbridge next to the road bridge, which opens on 1 June.

The number 14 bus will use the diversion and add ten minutes to its timetable. The bus company said that it will run two extra buses in the morning peak so that the service stays every 15 minutes.

Shops worry about trade

Traders on Tanner Road said that the closure comes at a bad time. Wendy Osei, who runs the hardware shop next to the bridge, said that about a third of her customers drive in from the north estate. The county offered free parking in the Mill Street car park for the six weeks.

The work costs 1.1 million pounds. A county spokesperson said the contractor works seven days a week to keep the closure as short as possible, and that a night closure would have taken five months.
