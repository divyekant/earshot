Workshop technician (bicycles), full time

Posted 6 February · Reference CW-24-07

Cogwheel is a worker cooperative that repairs and sells bicycles in Eastgate. We are looking for a technician to join our workshop team of five.

What you will do

  • Service and repair all kinds of bicycles, including electric bikes and cargo bikes
  • Build wheels and fit new parts
  • Explain repairs to customers and give honest quotes
  • Teach one evening class each month for our members

What we offer

The salary is £27,500 a year for 37.5 hours a week, the same rate for every member of the cooperative. After six months you can become a member with a vote. You get 30 days of holiday including public holidays, and a budget of £300 a year for tools.

How to apply

Send a short letter and your work history to the hiring group by 1 March. Tell us about one repair that you are proud of. Interviews include a practical test of one hour in the workshop.
