Gallery 3 · 12 April to 29 September

Paper Boats: folded ships from 1890 to today

This exhibition shows more than 200 paper boats, from a schoolboy's newspaper boat of 1893 to a three-metre model of a sailing ship folded from one sheet. Many were made by sailors on long voyages, who folded boats from charts, letters and labels.

The largest piece is the "Ellen Varley", a model of a tea clipper that the folder Mattias Rehn made over four winters. It needed 1,100 hours of work and no glue at all.

In the last room, visitors can fold their own boat and float it on a long water table. Paper and instructions are free.
