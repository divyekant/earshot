Year 4 trip to Hollow Wood Outdoor Centre

Dear parents and carers,

On Wednesday 15 May, Year 4 will visit Hollow Wood Outdoor Centre for a day of den building, pond dipping and a woodland walk. This trip is part of our science topic on living things and their habitats.

The coach leaves school at 8:45 and comes back at about 15:15. Please make sure your child is in school by 8:30.

The cost of the trip is £14. Please pay through the school payment app by Friday 3 May. If the cost is a problem, speak to Mrs Adebayo in the office in confidence.

Your child needs a packed lunch in a bag they can carry, a water bottle, a waterproof coat and old shoes or wellies. No sweets or fizzy drinks, please.

We need four parent helpers. If you can help, tell your child's class teacher by 3 May.

Yours sincerely,

Mr T. Okonjo, Year 4 leader
