Calder Print		 Winter Concert 2026: "Light in the Dark"

Our winter concert is on Saturday 12 December 2026 at 19:30 in St Aldhelm's Church, Church Row. The doors open at 18:45.

The choir of 64 singers will perform winter music from five centuries, with the Calder Brass Quintet and the organist Ffion Castellan. The conductor is Marek Oyelaran. The concert lasts about two hours with one interval.

Programme

Part oneMotets by Byrd and Praetorius; "A Ceremony of Carols" (selection) with harp
Interval20 minutes. Hot drinks and mince pies in the church hall
Part twoCarols for choir and brass; three new songs by local school children; carols for all to sing

Tickets

Tickets cost £14 for adults and £6 for people under 18. Children under 8 come in free but need a ticket. You can buy tickets at Fenner's Bookshop on Cheap Street, from each choir member, or at the door if seats are left. Last year all 320 seats were sold nine days before the concert.

All of the profit goes to the Sorrel Valley Food Bank. Last year the concert raised £2,870.

Getting there

There is no car park at the church. Use the Market Place car park, which is free after 18:00 and five minutes on foot. The church has a ramp at the north door and a hearing loop. Please tell us before 5 December if you need a wheelchair space: phone 01632 960 114.
