Head Gardener, Wrenfield Hall
Full time
Permanent
£34,500 a year
Posted 6 days ago
Ref GR-20418
About the job
Wrenfield Hall is a private estate with 14 acres of gardens, open to visitors from April to October. We look for a Head Gardener to lead a team of three gardeners and about twenty volunteers.
You will plan and do the work in the walled kitchen garden, the long borders and the arboretum. You will also manage the yearly plant budget of £18,000 and give four public garden tours each season.
What we ask
• A horticulture qualification at level 3, or the same in experience
• At least 5 years of work in a garden that is open to the public, 2 of them as a team leader
• A certificate for the use of pesticides (PA1 and PA6)
• A full driving licence
• Experience with fruit trees in trained forms is an advantage
What we offer
The salary is £34,500 a year for 39 hours a week. You work one weekend in four from April to October. There are 25 days of holiday plus public holidays, and a pension to which the estate pays 6 percent. A cottage with two bedrooms on the estate is available at a reduced rent of £450 a month.
How to apply
Send your CV and a letter of no more than two pages to the Estate Manager, Halima Brandt, at jobs@wrenfield.example. The closing date is Friday 27 November 2026 at 17:00. Interviews are on 8 and 9 December at the Hall and include a practical task of one hour. We hope that the new Head Gardener can start on 1 February 2027.
