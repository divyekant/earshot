Route 14: diversion in Millgate from 16 November
Published 10 November 2026

From Monday 16 November until about Friday 11 December, Tanner Street is closed for gas main work between Weir Lane and the market hall. Route 14 buses in the two directions will go along Foundry Road.

The stops Tanner Street North, Market Hall and Cheap Street are not served. Use the temporary stop on Foundry Road outside the tool hire shop, or the stop at Alder Square. The first and the last bus of the day stay at the same times, but trips can be up to 6 minutes late.

Route 14A is not changed.
