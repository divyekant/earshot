Letter to parents and carers: November 2026

Friday 6 November 2026

Dear parents and carers,

Thank you to all who came to the parents' evenings last week. More than nine in ten families came, which is the best number that we have had.

Swimming for Classes 3 and 4. Swimming lessons at Harrowmere Leisure Centre start on Wednesday 18 November and continue for six weeks. The coach leaves at 13:10 and is back by 15:15. Each child needs a swimsuit, a towel and a swimming hat in a named bag. We ask for a voluntary payment of £15 for the coach. Please pay on Parent Pay by Monday 16 November.

Winter fair. The winter fair is on Friday 4 December from 15:30 to 18:00 in the school hall. The parents' association needs helpers for the stalls and cakes for the cake table. If you can help for an hour, please write your name on the list at the office.

Class photographs. The photographer comes on Tuesday 24 November. Children must wear the full school uniform that day. Order forms come home in the bags on the same day, and the last day for orders is 2 December.

Cold weather. The children go outside at each break if it does not rain hard. Please send a warm coat every day, with a name in it. We have forty coats with no name in lost property.

Last day of term. School ends at 13:30 on Friday 18 December. There is no after-school club on that day. The spring term starts on Tuesday 5 January 2027.

With best wishes,
Mrs Folasade Whitcombe
Headteacher
