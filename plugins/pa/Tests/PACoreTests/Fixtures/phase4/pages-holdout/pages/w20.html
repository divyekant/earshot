Apply for a resident parking permit

If you live in a controlled parking zone, you need a resident permit to park on the street in the hours that the signs show. A permit is for one vehicle and one zone.

Who can apply

You can apply if the address in the zone is your main home and the vehicle is registered at that address. Each household can have 2 permits at most. You cannot get a permit for a vehicle that is longer than 5.25 metres or heavier than 3.5 tonnes.

What it costs

Permit12 months6 months
First permit, electric vehicle£35£20
First permit, other vehicle£96£52
Second permit£164£88

Blue Badge holders do not pay for their first permit.

What you need

  • The vehicle registration certificate (log book) with your address in the zone
  • One proof of address from the last 3 months, for example a council tax bill or a bank statement
  • A debit or credit card
If you moved in a short time ago and your log book still has your old address, you can get a temporary permit for 8 weeks. You must send the new log book before the 8 weeks end, or the permit stops.

How long it takes

We check most applications in 5 working days. Your permit is digital: we do not send a paper permit, and you do not need to show a thing in the vehicle. You can park as soon as you get the confirmation email.

Renew your permit before it ends. We send a reminder by email 4 weeks before the end date. If the permit ends, you can get a penalty charge of £70.
