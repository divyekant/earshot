Fieldnote 5.4 Latest

Released on 27 October 2026 for macOS, Windows, iOS and Android

Version 5.4 brings offline maps for a full region, a faster sync and a new export to spreadsheets. It needs macOS 13, Windows 10, iOS 16 or Android 11, or later.

Download for macOS (84 MB) · Windows (91 MB) · All platforms

New

  • Offline maps: download a full region, up to 2 GB, and not only the area on the screen.
  • Export a notebook to an .xlsx file with one row for each observation.
  • Voice notes now show a text version on iOS and Android. The text is made on the device.
  • A dark map style for work at night.

Changed

  • Sync is about 4 times faster for notebooks with more than 1,000 photos, because the app now sends only the changed parts.
  • The photo limit for one observation is now 30. It was 12.
  • The coordinate format setting moved from the map menu to Settings > Units.
  • The free plan now includes 3 notebooks. It was 2.

Fixed

  • The app could close when you rotated the phone during a GPS fix.
  • Dates before 1970 showed as 1 January 1970 in exports.
  • On Windows, the search did not find words with accents.
  • A shared notebook sometimes showed the wrong owner.
  • Battery use in the background on Android 14 was too high.

Known problems

On macOS 13, the dark map style shows contour lines too light. A fix comes in 5.4.1. Until then, use the standard style for work with contour lines.

Before you update

Version 5.4 changes the format of the local database. After the update, an older version of the app cannot open your notebooks. If you use Fieldnote on more than one device, update all of them before you start a sync.
