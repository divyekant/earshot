Boiler loses pressure every two days - no visible leak
Posted: Sat 24 Oct 2026, 09:12
kestrel_way
New member
Posts: 3
Joined: Oct 2026		 Hello all. Our combi boiler is 9 years old. For the last three weeks the pressure gauge drops from 1.4 bar to about 0.6 bar in two days, and then the boiler locks out with fault F22. I fill it up with the filling loop and it works again.

I checked every radiator valve and all the pipes that I can see. Nothing is wet. There is no stain on any ceiling. The pressure falls also when the heating is off for the whole day.

Where can the water go if there is no leak? Do I need a new boiler?

Old_Flux

Posts: 4,812
Joined: Mar 2014		 You do not need a new boiler yet. With no wet patch in the house, there are two usual causes.

First, the expansion vessel. If it lost its air charge, the pressure goes up very high when the heating runs, and the pressure relief valve lets water out through the small copper pipe on the outside wall. Tie a plastic bag over the end of that pipe for a day. If there is water in the bag, that is your answer.

Second, the heat exchanger can leak into the condensate drain. Then the water goes down the plastic waste pipe and you never see it.
Gas work is for registered engineers. I only give general advice.

Joined: Jan 2021		 We had the same last winter. Watch the gauge while the heating is on. Ours went from 1.2 to 3 bar in twenty minutes. The engineer pumped the vessel up again to 0.9 bar with a foot pump at the valve under the boiler. It cost us £85 and took half an hour. No problem since then.
