Questions about pricing

What happens when the trial ends?

We ask you to select a plan. If you do not select one, your account becomes read only. We keep your data for 90 days, and you can export it at any time in that period.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can go up or down at any time. If you go up, you pay the difference for the days that are left in the month.

How do I cancel?

Go to Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan. There is no minimum term for a monthly plan. A yearly plan is not refunded after the first 14 days.

Do you charge for extra users?

Each user above the limit of your plan costs £4 each month.
