Hire terms and conditions
Version 7, valid from 1 July 2026
These terms apply to each hire of equipment from Brackwell Tool Hire Ltd ("we") by a customer ("you"). Read them before you sign the hire contract.
1. The hire period. The hire starts when the equipment leaves our branch and ends when it is back at the branch or when we collect it. The minimum hire is one day. A day is 24 hours from the start time on the contract. A weekend hire, from Saturday 12:00 to Monday 08:30, counts as one and a half days.
2. Charges and deposit. You pay the hire charge of our price list plus VAT. For a customer with no trade account we take a deposit before the hire: £50 for equipment with a value below £500, and £150 for all other equipment. We return the deposit in 5 working days after the equipment is back, clean and with no damage.
3. Late return. If you keep the equipment after the agreed end, the hire continues at the daily rate. We do not send a reminder. If the equipment is more than 7 days late and you did not contact us, we can treat it as lost.
Need it for longer? Ask at the counter for our 4-week rate and save up to 40%.
4. Your duties. You must use the equipment as the instructions say and only for the work it is made for. You must not lend it to another person or repair it yourself. You must clean it before the return. If we must clean it, we charge £25.
5. Breakdown. If the equipment stops, tell us at once by phone. We repair or change it as fast as we can, and you do not pay for the time in which you could not use it. This does not apply if the cause is wrong use, wrong fuel or a puncture.
6. Loss and damage. You are responsible for the equipment from the start to the end of the hire. If it is lost, stolen or damaged beyond repair, you pay the cost of new equipment of the same type, and the hire charge continues until you pay. For theft you must give us a crime reference number in 48 hours.
7. Damage waiver. You can add a damage waiver for 12 percent of the hire charge. With the waiver, you pay only the first £100 of each accidental damage. The waiver does not cover theft, loss, tyres or damage from wrong use.
8. Cancellation. You can cancel a booking free of charge up to 16:00 on the working day before the hire starts. After that time we charge one day of hire.
9. Our liability. We are not liable for loss of profit or for delay of your work. Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or injury caused by our negligence.
10. Complaints. Write to the branch manager first. If you do not get an answer in 14 days, write to Customer Care, Brackwell Tool Hire Ltd, Unit 9 Foundry Road.
