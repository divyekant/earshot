Fennick Dale 1.7 L Gooseneck Kettle, matt green

The Dale is a stovetop kettle with a narrow gooseneck spout for slow, exact pouring. It is made for pour-over coffee, but the 1.7 litre body is large enough for a family teapot.

The body is stainless steel with a matt enamel coat. The base has three layers and works on gas, electric, ceramic and induction hobs. A thermometer in the lid shows the water temperature from 40 to 100 °C, so that you can stop at 93 °C for coffee or at 80 °C for green tea.

The handle is beech wood and stays cool. Do not put the kettle in a dishwasher: the wood and the enamel can be damaged. Fill it to the mark inside, not to the top, or water can come out of the spout when it boils.
