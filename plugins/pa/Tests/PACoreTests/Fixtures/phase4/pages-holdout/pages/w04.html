Port Elin trip – plan

Dates: Friday 5 March to Monday 8 March 2027. Three nights. Travelling with Jonas and Ines.

Travel

Train from Dunmarrow at 07:42, arrives Port Elin 11:05, one change at Carrow Junction with 14 minutes. Booked, reference K7QM2X, £126 for the three of us with the group ticket. The way back is open: any train on Monday after 10:00.

Where we stay

The Harbour Loft, 2 Net Quay. Two rooms, £310 in total for three nights. Check-in from 15:00, so we need a place for the bags until then. The owner said by mail that we can leave them in the café below from 11:00. Pay the rest of £210 on arrival, by card.

Things to do

  • Saturday: boat to Gull Island, leaves 10:15 from the east pier, back at 16:00. £22 each. Does not go if the wind is above force 6. Book the day before at the kiosk.
  • Sunday morning: the tide is low at 09:20, so we can walk to the lighthouse on the causeway. We must be back on the town side before 11:45.
  • Sunday afternoon: maritime museum, closes 17:00, free on the first Sunday of the month.
  • Fish dinner at The Salt Cellar on Saturday, table for three at 19:30. Ines made the booking.

Still to do

  • Ask Jonas to bring the big rucksack
  • Check that the boat runs in March on Saturdays, the website only shows the summer times
  • Buy seasickness tablets
  • Give the spare key to Mrs Achterberg for the plants

Budget for each person: about £260 with food. I paid the train and the deposit of £100, so Jonas and Ines owe me £75.33 each so far.
