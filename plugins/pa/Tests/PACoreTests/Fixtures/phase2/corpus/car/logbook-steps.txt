How to tell the licensing agency that you sold your car.

You must tell the agency the same day that you sell the car. You can do it online with the 11-digit reference number from the logbook, or by post with the yellow section of the logbook.

Online: enter the reference number, the registration number, and the name and address of the buyer. You get an email to confirm. The buyer gets the new logbook within five working days.

By post: fill in the yellow slip with the name and address of the buyer and send it to the agency. Give the rest of the logbook to the buyer. The buyer fills in nothing.

After you tell the agency, you get a refund of the vehicle tax for every full month that is left. You do not need to apply for it. The refund goes to the address on the logbook, so make sure that it is correct.

Cancel the car insurance from the day after the sale. Keep a copy of the sale receipt with the date, the price, and the signatures of both people.
