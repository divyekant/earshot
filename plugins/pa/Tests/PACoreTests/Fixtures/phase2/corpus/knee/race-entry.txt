Entry confirmation, Riverside Half Marathon.

Thank you for your entry. Race date: Sunday 26 April. Start at 9.00 at the town hall. The course follows the river, two laps, mostly flat.

Your race number and timing chip come by post two weeks before the race. If you cannot run, you can transfer your place to another runner until 31 March for a fee of 10 pounds. After that, transfers are not possible and there are no refunds.

There are water stations at 5, 10 and 15 kilometres, and energy gels at 15 kilometres. Bag drop is at the leisure centre.

The time limit is three hours. Runners slower than the limit must move to the pavement.

Please train safely. If you have an injury, talk to a doctor or a physiotherapist before the race.

Your race number and the timing chip come by post two weeks before the race. Fix the number on the front of your top. Do not swap numbers with other runners.
