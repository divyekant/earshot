Treatment plan from Westside Physiotherapy.

Problem: pain at the front of the right knee after running, for about two months. The tests show irritation of the tendon below the kneecap. There is no sign of damage to the ligaments.

Plan: six sessions over eight weeks. In the sessions we use manual therapy, taping and a programme of exercises. The main part is the exercises at home.

Home exercises, every day: wall sits, three times 45 seconds. Slow single leg squats on a step, three times eight. Calf raises, three times fifteen. Stretches for the thigh and the hip, twice a day.

Running: stop for two weeks. Then start again with walk and run intervals on flat ground, 20 minutes, every second day. Pain up to 3 out of 10 is acceptable during the run, if it is gone by the next morning.

Ice for ten minutes after the exercises can help. Do not take pain killers before a run to hide the pain.
