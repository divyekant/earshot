Letter from the surgery to the physiotherapy service.

I saw this patient today with pain in the right knee after running, for about eight weeks. There was no injury or fall. The pain is at the front of the knee, below the kneecap, and is worse on stairs and after sitting for a long time.

On examination, the knee was not swollen, the movement was full, and the ligaments were stable. There was tenderness at the lower edge of the kneecap.

I think this is a tendon problem from running more than usual, in training for a half marathon. I do not think an x-ray is needed now.

Please see the patient for assessment and treatment. The patient takes no regular medicines and has no allergies.

If there is no improvement after eight weeks of treatment, please send the patient back to me, and we will think about a scan.
