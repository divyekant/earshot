Rental income and costs for the flat on Mill Lane, for the tax year.

Rent received: twelve months at 950 pounds, a total of 11,400 pounds. The tenant paid every month on time. The deposit is held in the protection scheme and is not income.

Costs. Letting agent fees, at ten percent of the rent: 1,140 pounds. Repair of the boiler in February: 380 pounds. New lock on the front door: 95 pounds. Landlord insurance: 210 pounds. Ground rent and service charge: 640 pounds. Gas safety check: 70 pounds.

Total costs: 2,535 pounds. Profit before tax: 8,865 pounds.

The mortgage interest is not a cost in this list. It gives a tax credit at the basic rate. The interest for the year was 2,980 pounds, from the annual mortgage statement.
