Home office notes for the tax return.

I worked from home on Mondays and Fridays from April to the end of September, and every day from October to December, because the office was closed for building work. In total, that is about 34 weeks with some days at home.

The simple rate for working from home is a flat amount each week. The other way is to work out the real share of the costs of heating, electricity and broadband, but that needs the bills and a calculation of the room size. Helen says the flat rate is easier and the difference is small.

My employer did not pay me an allowance for working from home. So I can claim it myself.

I bought a desk chair for 180 pounds and a second screen for 140 pounds. The employer did not pay for them. Ask Helen if they count.

The broadband bill is in my name and costs 32 pounds a month.
