Your stay at Casa Azulejo, Alfama, Lisbon.

Check-in from 15.00 on Saturday 14 March. Check-out by 11.00 on Saturday 21 March. Seven nights, two guests.

The apartment is on the third floor of an old building with no lift. It has one bedroom, a small kitchen, a bathroom with a shower, and a balcony with a view of the river.

Self check-in: the code for the key box will be sent by message on the day of arrival. The key box is to the right of the blue door. If you arrive before 15.00, you can leave your bags at the luggage shop on the corner.

The tourist tax is two euros per person per night, paid in cash on arrival, in the envelope on the table.

Please separate the rubbish: glass, paper and plastic go in the street bins. Quiet hours are from 22.00 to 08.00. No smoking inside.

Total paid: 742 euros. Free cancellation until 30 days before arrival.
