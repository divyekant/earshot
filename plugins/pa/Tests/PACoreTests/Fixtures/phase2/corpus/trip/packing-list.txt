Packing list for Lisbon in March.

Clothes: light jacket, one warm jumper, a rain jacket, two pairs of trousers, one pair of shorts in case of sun, t-shirts, walking shoes with good grip, because the streets are steep and the stones are slippery when wet.

Documents: passports, the flight booking, travel insurance policy, the European health card, a copy of the apartment booking, and some euros in cash for the tourist tax.

Electronics: phone and charger, a travel adapter for European plugs, the small power bank, headphones.

Other: sun cream, sunglasses, a small backpack for day trips, a reusable water bottle, and a phrase book.

Before we leave: tell the bank about the trip, set the out-of-office message at work, ask the neighbour to water the plants, and turn the heating down.

Hand luggage only for the children, one cabin bag each. Put the medicines and the chargers in the cabin bags, not in the big case, in case the case arrives late.
