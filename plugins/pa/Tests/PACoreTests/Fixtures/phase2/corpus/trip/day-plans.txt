Ideas for the days in Lisbon.

Sunday: the castle in the morning before the crowds, then walk down through Alfama. Lunch at a small place with grilled sardines. In the evening, a fado show; book a table in advance.

Monday: tram 28 early in the morning. Then the Gulbenkian museum and its garden. Many museums close on Mondays, so check first.

Tuesday: a day trip to Sintra by train from Rossio station. Visit the Pena palace and the Moorish castle. Buy the palace tickets online to skip the queue. Wear good shoes.

Wednesday: Belem. The monastery, the tower, and the custard tarts at the old bakery. The queue for the tarts moves fast.

Thursday: a rest day. The beach at Cascais by train along the coast, about 40 minutes.

Friday: the LX Factory market, shopping, and a last dinner with a view at a rooftop bar.

Saturday: pack in the morning, leave the bags at the flat, and walk in the Estrela garden before the taxi to the airport. Keep some cash for the tram.
