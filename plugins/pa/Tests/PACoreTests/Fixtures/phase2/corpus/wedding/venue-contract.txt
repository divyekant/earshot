Booking agreement, The Old Mill, for the wedding of Sam Reed and Lea Novak.

Date: Saturday 6 June. Ceremony at 14.00 in the barn. Drinks on the lawn from 15.00. Dinner at 18.00 in the hall for up to 90 guests. Evening party until midnight, for up to 130 guests.

Price for the venue: 6,500 pounds. Catering: 72 pounds per guest for dinner, 14 pounds per guest for the evening food. Drinks are paid separately from the bar list.

Deposit: 2,000 pounds, paid. Second payment of half the rest: three months before the date. Final payment and final guest numbers: four weeks before the date.

Cancellation: if you cancel more than six months before the date, you lose the deposit only. Later, the payments made are not returned.

Rules: no confetti made of plastic. Candles only in glass holders. The band must stop at 23.30. Guests must leave the site by 00.30.
