Notes from the second visit to 5 Orchard Close.

The boiler is in the garage. It was serviced last year. The stopcock for the water is under the kitchen sink. The fuse box is in the cupboard under the stairs.

The smoke alarms in the hall and on the landing need new batteries. There is no carbon monoxide alarm near the boiler; buy one.

The bedroom at the back is the quietest. The small room can be the office; it has a phone socket and gets the morning sun.

The garden has a lawn, a small patio and a broken gate that needs a new hinge.

The seller leaves the curtains in the living room and the fridge. The washing machine goes with them.

The bins are collected on Tuesday. Recycling every two weeks.

The neighbours on the left, Tom and Ellie, have a dog and were friendly.
