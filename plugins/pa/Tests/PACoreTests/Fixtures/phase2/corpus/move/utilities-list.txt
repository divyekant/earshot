Things to tell about the move.

Electricity and gas: give the meter readings on the day of the move at both addresses. Take photos of the meters. The supplier for the new house is not known yet; the seller says it is a small company.

Water: tell the water company the move date. The new house has a water meter.

Broadband: the contract runs until August. The provider can move the service to the new address, but it takes two weeks. Book it now.

Council tax: tell both councils. The new house is in band D.

Post: set up the redirection for twelve months. It costs about 80 pounds.

Also tell: the bank, the doctor, the dentist, the car insurance and the licensing agency for the driving licence, the employer, and the school if needed.

TV licence: move it online.

Read the meters on the day we leave and on the day we arrive, and take a photo of each meter. Send the readings to the suppliers the same day.
