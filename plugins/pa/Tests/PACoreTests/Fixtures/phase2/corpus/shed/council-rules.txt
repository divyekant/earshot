Do I need planning permission for a garden building?

Most garden sheds do not need planning permission. A shed is permitted development if it meets these rules.

It is not in front of the house. It has a single storey. Its height is not more than 2.5 metres if it is within 2 metres of a boundary. Otherwise, the eaves are not more than 2.5 metres and the total height is not more than 4 metres for a dual pitched roof, or 3 metres for other roofs. Together with other buildings, it covers not more than half of the garden.

It is not used to live or sleep in.

Different rules apply to a listed building, to a house in a conservation area, and to a flat.

If you are not sure, you can ask the council for a lawful development certificate. The fee is half of the fee for planning permission. Building regulations do not apply to most sheds under 15 square metres with no sleeping room.
