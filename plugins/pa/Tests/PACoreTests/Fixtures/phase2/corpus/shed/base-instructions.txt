How to build a base for a garden shed.

A shed needs a base that is flat, level and firm. The three common types are a concrete slab, paving slabs on sand and cement, and a plastic grid filled with gravel.

For a shed of 8 by 6 feet, make the base a little bigger, about 2.6 by 2 metres. Mark the area with pegs and string. Dig out the turf and about 10 centimetres of soil. Lay a membrane to stop weeds.

For paving slabs: spread a layer of sharp sand mixed with cement, about 5 centimetres deep. Lay the slabs on it and tap them down with a rubber mallet. Check the level in both directions with a long spirit level. Leave a small gap between the slabs.

For a concrete slab: build a timber frame, fill it with hardcore, then pour concrete about 10 centimetres deep. Let it cure for at least a week before the shed goes on it.

The base should be a few centimetres above the ground, so that rain runs off.
