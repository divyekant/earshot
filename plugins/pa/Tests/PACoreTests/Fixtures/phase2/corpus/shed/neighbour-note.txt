Note from our neighbour, Mrs Avery, at number 14.

Hello, I saw the pegs and string at the end of your garden and I guess that you plan a shed. That is fine with me, but I have two small requests.

First, the old apple tree on the boundary is mine, and its roots go under the fence. Please do not cut the big roots when you dig the base, because the tree is very old and I want to keep it.

Second, could the shed be a little away from the fence, perhaps half a metre? Then the water from the roof does not run into my vegetable bed, and you can still reach the back of the shed to paint it.

If you want, you can use my side garden gate on the day of the delivery. It is wider than yours. Just knock on the door.

Best wishes, Joan Avery
