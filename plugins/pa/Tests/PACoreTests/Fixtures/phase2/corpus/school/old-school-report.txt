End of year report for Mia, Year 3, Hillcrest Primary.

Mia is a kind and helpful member of the class. She works well with other children and often helps new pupils find their way.

Reading: Mia reads with confidence and enjoys stories about animals. She should read more non-fiction to build her vocabulary. Writing: her handwriting has improved a lot. She plans her stories well but sometimes forgets full stops. Maths: Mia is strong in number work and knows her times tables up to eight. She finds word problems harder and needs to read the question twice.

Science: she asked good questions in our topic about plants and kept a careful diary of the bean experiment. Music: she sings in the school choir and started the recorder this year.

Attendance: 96 percent. She was late to school four times.

Targets for next year: use full stops and capital letters every time, read one non-fiction book each month, and practise the 9 and 12 times tables.
