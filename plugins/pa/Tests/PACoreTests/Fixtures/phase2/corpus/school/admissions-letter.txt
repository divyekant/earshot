Admissions office, Riverside Primary School.

Thank you for your application for a place in Year 4 for your daughter Mia, starting in January.

We have a place in Year 4. To accept the place, please return the signed form within ten school days. With the form, we need a copy of the birth certificate, proof of your address such as a council tax bill, and the last school report from her current school.

Mia can visit the school for half a day before she starts. Her class teacher will be Mr Daniels. The visit days are on Wednesdays in December. Please call the office to book a time.

School starts at 8.45 and ends at 3.15. The breakfast club opens at 7.45 and the after-school club runs until 5.30. Both clubs need a separate booking and a fee.

The school uniform is a navy jumper with the school logo, a white polo shirt and grey trousers or skirt. You can buy the jumper from the school office or from the uniform shop on Bridge Street.

We look forward to meeting Mia.
