School bus route 7, Riverside Primary, winter timetable.

The bus leaves the stop at Oak Avenue at 8.05, then stops at the library at 8.12, at the park gate at 8.20, and arrives at the school at 8.35. In the afternoon, the bus leaves the school at 3.25 and arrives back at Oak Avenue at 3.55.

Children under eight must be met at the stop in the afternoon by an adult. Children from eight can walk home alone if a parent signs the travel form.

A bus pass for one term costs 120 pounds. Families with a low income can apply for a free pass. The pass must be shown to the driver every day.

On days with snow or ice, the bus company sends a text message by 7.00 if the bus does not run. Make sure that the school has your mobile number.

Lost property on the bus goes to the school office on the next day.
