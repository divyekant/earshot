Uniform and kit list for Riverside Primary.

Every day: a navy jumper or cardigan with the logo, a white polo shirt, grey trousers, a grey skirt or a grey pinafore, black school shoes, and a navy coat in winter. In summer, a blue checked dress is also allowed.

PE kit, in a named bag that stays at school from Monday to Friday: a navy t-shirt, navy shorts, plain trainers, and a navy tracksuit for outdoor PE in winter.

Other things: a book bag with the logo, a water bottle with the name on it, and a pair of wellington boots for the forest school on Thursdays. Forest school also needs a waterproof jacket and trousers.

Please put a name label in every piece of clothing. Clothes with no name that are left behind go to the lost property box. The box is emptied at the end of each half term, and clothes that nobody collects go to the second-hand uniform sale.

Jewellery is not allowed, except small stud earrings.
