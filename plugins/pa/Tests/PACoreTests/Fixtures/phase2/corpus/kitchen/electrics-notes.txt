Notes from the visit of the electrician, Ravi Okafor, about the kitchen.

The kitchen needs a new circuit for the induction hob. The hob takes up to 7.4 kilowatts, so the old cooker circuit is too small. Ravi will run a new cable from the consumer unit in the hall, under the floor, to a new isolator switch next to the hob.

We want six double sockets along the worktop, two of them with USB. The sockets go 15 centimetres above the worktop. The extractor fan above the hob needs a fused spur. Under-cabinet lights need a transformer and a switch near the door.

The consumer unit is old but has spare ways. Ravi says it is fine for now, but he advises a new unit within five years.

The work must happen after the plumber and before the plasterer. Ravi needs about three days. He will test and certify the new circuits at the end, and he gives us the certificate for the building control file.

Price estimate: 1,150 pounds including the cable, the sockets and the certificate. The lights are extra, about 120 pounds.
