Valley Wheelers newsletter, spring.

The new season starts on the first Sunday of April. Club rides leave the café on the green at 9.00. There are three groups: the fast group, about 28 kilometres per hour, 80 kilometres; the steady group, about 24, 60 kilometres; and the social group, about 20, 40 kilometres with a café stop.

Membership for the year is 35 pounds, or 20 pounds for juniors. It includes insurance for third party claims. Pay online by the end of March to stay on the ride list.

Club kit: the new jersey design is ready. Orders close on 15 March. A short sleeve jersey is 45 pounds, bib shorts 55 pounds.

Rules on club rides: a helmet is required, mudguards from October to March, lights in bad weather, and no headphones.

Volunteers needed for the hill climb in June: marshals and timekeepers. Contact Gwen.

Welcome to the new members who joined this winter. The Saturday social ride leaves the cafe at nine and waits for everyone at the top of each hill. New riders are always welcome.
