Minutes of the Valley Wheelers committee meeting.

Present: Gwen (chair), Marcus (treasurer), Dee (secretary), Olu (ride captain), and two members.

1. Money. Marcus reported 2,340 pounds in the bank. The insurance renewal costs 610 pounds and is due in April. The club kit order needs a deposit of 300 pounds.

2. Rides. Olu proposed a new beginner group in the summer, every second Saturday, 30 kilometres. Agreed. Olu needs two ride leaders for it.

3. Hill climb in June. Gwen has the permission from the council. We still need eight marshals and two timekeepers. Dee will email the members.

4. Safety. A member fell on the gravel at the bottom of Mill Hill. The committee will write to the council about the road surface. Ride leaders should warn the groups at that place.

5. Next meeting: the first Tuesday of next month, in the back room of the Fox.

Any other business: the club will buy two new first aid kits for the ride leaders. Gwen thanked the members who helped at the winter clean-up of the clubhouse.
