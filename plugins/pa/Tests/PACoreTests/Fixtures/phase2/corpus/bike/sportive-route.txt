Route notes for the Three Valleys sportive, Saturday 9 May.

Distance: 112 kilometres with 1,650 metres of climbing. Start at the village hall from 7.30. Check in with photo ID and the confirmation email.

The first 30 kilometres are flat along the river. Then the long climb to the reservoir, 6 kilometres at an average of 5 percent. First feed station at the reservoir dam, 42 kilometres: water, bananas, cake.

From there a fast descent with sharp bends; the road surface is poor on the lower part. Then two short steep climbs, the second one at 14 percent near the old church.

Second feed station at 78 kilometres in the market square. The last section is rolling and finishes with a sprint to the village hall.

The time limit is 8 hours. A broom wagon follows the last riders. Take two bottles, a spare tube, a pump and a light rain jacket.
