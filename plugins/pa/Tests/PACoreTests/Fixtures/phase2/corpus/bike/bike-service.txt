Workshop report from Cog and Chain.

Bike: a road bike with an aluminium frame and a carbon fork, 11 speed.

Work done: full service. Cleaned and degreased the drivetrain. Replaced the chain, which was worn past the limit. Checked the cassette; it is still fine but will need replacing after the next chain. Replaced the rear brake pads. Adjusted the gears and the brakes. Trued the rear wheel. Checked all bolts to the right torque.

Advice: the front tyre has a cut in the tread. It is safe for now, but replace it before long rides. The bar tape is worn; new tape costs 20 pounds.

Parts: chain 32 pounds, brake pads 18 pounds. Labour for the full service: 75 pounds.

Total: 125 pounds.

Next service in about 3,000 kilometres or six months.

We checked the wheels for true and the spokes for tension. Both wheels are fine. The bike is ready to collect from Friday after ten in the morning.
