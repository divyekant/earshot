Minutes of the annual general meeting of the Westfield Allotment Society

Held in the church hall on Saturday at two o'clock. Present: 41 members and the committee. Apologies from 6 members. The chair, Margaret Osei, opened the meeting and thanked everyone for coming.

1. The report of the chair. Margaret said that it was a good year on the site. All 64 plots are let, and the waiting list has 23 names. The new water troughs were installed in April, and the shared shed got a new roof. She thanked the volunteers who cleared the old hedge by the gate.

2. The report of the treasurer. The society ends the year with 3,820 pounds in the bank, after it paid for the water troughs and the shed roof. The income from plot rents was 2,560 pounds. The shop for seeds and compost made a profit of 340 pounds. The biggest cost was the water bill, which rose by a third.

3. The rents. After a discussion, the members voted to raise the plot rent from 40 to 45 pounds a year, from next April, mainly to pay for the higher water bill. Half plots go from 20 to 23 pounds. Members over 70 keep their discount of one quarter. The vote was 33 for, 5 against, and 3 who did not vote.

4. Plots in poor condition. The committee reported that 5 plots were not worked this year. The holders got letters in July. Three holders have since cleared their plots. The other two will get a final letter, and if nothing changes in 30 days, the plots go to people on the waiting list.

5. The security of the site. There were two break-ins at sheds this summer, and some tools were taken. A member offered to fit a better lock on the main gate. The meeting agreed to buy a new gate lock and to give each member a new key in return for a deposit of 5 pounds. The committee will also ask the police for advice.

6. Election of the committee. Margaret Osei was elected as chair again. Sam Lindqvist stays as treasurer. Priti Nair is the new secretary, as Joan Webb stands down after nine years. The meeting thanked Joan with a long round of applause and a gift voucher for the garden centre.

7. Any other business. A member asked about a composting toilet on the site. The committee will get prices and report at the next meeting. Another member suggested a plant swap in May. Several members offered to help.

The meeting closed at four o'clock with tea and cake. The next committee meeting is on the first Wednesday of next month.
