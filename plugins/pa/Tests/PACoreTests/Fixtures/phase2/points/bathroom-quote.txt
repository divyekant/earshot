Quote for the new bathroom at 22 Linden Road

From: Carter and Sons, bathrooms and tiling. Quote number CS-3108. Valid for 60 days.

Thank you for inviting us to look at your bathroom. This quote follows our visit last Thursday and the list of wishes that you gave us.

We propose to remove the old bath and fit a walk-in shower with a glass screen in its place, with a new basin, a new toilet and a heated towel rail. The bathroom is about 2.2 by 1.9 metres, which is enough for a shower tray of 1,400 by 800 millimetres.

Stage one is the strip-out. We remove the bath, the basin, the toilet, the old tiles and the old flooring, and we take all the waste away in a skip. We check the floor boards and replace any that are soft. We expect some soft boards near the old toilet.

Stage two is the plumbing. We move the waste pipe for the shower, fit a new thermostatic mixer valve in the wall, and connect the towel rail to the heating. The towel rail also has an electric element for the summer.

Stage three is the walls and floor. We board the shower area with waterproof boards and tank it with a membrane. We tile the walls to the ceiling in the shower and to half height elsewhere. The floor gets vinyl planks that look like wood.

Stage four is the fitting and finishing. We fit the tray, the screen, the basin with a cabinet under it, the toilet, the towel rail, a mirror cabinet and an extractor fan. We seal all joints and leave the room clean.

The total price is 7,850 pounds, which includes all materials in the list, the labour, the skip and the electrical work for the fan and the towel rail. The tiles are included up to 35 pounds per square metre. If you choose more expensive tiles, we charge the difference only.

The work takes about twelve working days. The house has no other bathroom, so please plan for the days without a toilet; we keep the toilet working until day four and fit the new one by day seven.

We ask for a deposit of 20 percent to book a start date, a stage payment of 40 percent after the plumbing, and the rest when the work is complete and you are satisfied. We give a two year guarantee on our work. The products have the guarantees of their makers.

Possible extra costs: rot in the joists under the floor, or old pipes in lead that must be replaced. We will always tell you and agree a price before we do any extra work.

We hope to work with you. Call Dan Carter with any questions.
