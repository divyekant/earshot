Your home insurance renewal

Policy number HB-771204. Renewal date: 1 March. This letter tells you about your renewal and the changes for next year.

Your premium for next year is 486 pounds, which is 94 pounds more than this year. The main reason is a rise in the cost of repairs after storms and floods across the country. Your own record has not changed the price: you made no claim this year, and your no-claims discount stays at five years.

The policy covers the building and the contents of your home. The building cover is up to 350,000 pounds. The contents cover is up to 60,000 pounds. Single items worth more than 2,000 pounds must be listed by name, or they are covered only up to 2,000 pounds. At present, you have listed one item: a ring worth 3,200 pounds.

There are two changes to the terms this year. First, the excess for a claim for water damage from a leak rises from 250 pounds to 400 pounds. The excess for other claims stays at 150 pounds. Second, the policy no longer covers damage from a leak that was not repaired within 14 days after you knew about it.

If you want to keep the policy, you need to do nothing, because we will take the first monthly payment on 1 March from the same bank account. If your details have changed, please tell us before the renewal date. Changes that matter include a new roof, a lodger, a business at home, or a long time away from the house.

You can lower the premium. A higher voluntary excess of 250 pounds would save about 40 pounds a year. Fitting a lock to the standard of the insurers on all outside doors could save about 25 pounds. You can also remove the accidental damage cover, which costs 62 pounds a year, if you do not need it.

If you want to cancel or change the policy, call us before 1 March, or within 14 days after it, and you will get a full refund of any payment for the new year. After those 14 days, you can still cancel at any time, but we keep a charge for the days of cover and a fee of 25 pounds.

We recommend that you compare this offer with other insurers. Check that any other policy gives the same cover, and in particular the same limits for single items and the same excess for water damage.

The full terms are in the policy booklet, which you can read in your online account. If you need a printed copy, please ask.

Thank you for staying with us.

The customer team, Brightwell Home Insurance
