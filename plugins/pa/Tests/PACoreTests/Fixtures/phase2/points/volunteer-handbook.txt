Welcome to the Riverside Food Bank: a short handbook for new volunteers

Thank you for joining us. This handbook tells you what we do, how a shift works, and what to do if something goes wrong. Please read it before your first shift.

The food bank gives emergency food parcels to people in the town who are referred by a doctor, a school, a social worker or another partner. We do not ask people to prove that they are in need when they arrive. The referral is enough. Each parcel holds food for three days for the size of the household.

The food bank is open on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from ten until two. There are three kinds of shift. The warehouse shift sorts the donations, checks the dates and fills the shelves. The front shift welcomes guests, offers tea, and takes the referral voucher. The packing shift makes up the parcels from the voucher.

Every volunteer must sign in and sign out at the desk, because we need to know who is in the building in case of a fire. The fire exits are at the back of the warehouse and next to the kitchen. The meeting point is the car park across the road.

When you sort donations, check the date on each item. Food that is past its use-by date goes in the red bin. Food that is past its best-before date by less than three months can still go out, but put it at the front of the shelf. Tins that are badly dented, and packets that are open, go in the red bin.

Lifting: do not lift a box that feels too heavy. Ask someone to help, or use the trolley. Keep your back straight and bend your knees.

Talking with guests: be friendly, and keep what guests tell you private. Do not ask why they need the food bank. If a guest wants to talk, listen. If they need more help, such as with debt or housing, give them the advice leaflet from the front desk. Do not give your own advice about money or law.

If a guest is upset, angry or unwell, call the shift leader at once and do not try to handle the situation alone. The shift leader wears a green lanyard. There is a first aid kit in the kitchen, and the shift leader has the training to use it.

Please do not take photos inside the building, as guests may be in them. Do not post about guests on social media.

If you cannot come to a shift, tell the volunteer coordinator, Grace, as early as you can, by text or by email. We plan the shifts a week ahead.

We hope you enjoy your time with us. Thank you again.
