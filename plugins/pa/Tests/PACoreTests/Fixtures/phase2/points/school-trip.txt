Letter to parents: the Year 6 residential trip to Hollins Hall

Dear parents and carers,

We are pleased to tell you about the residential trip for Year 6 this summer term. The trip is to Hollins Hall outdoor centre in the hills, from Monday 9 June to Friday 13 June. It is a highlight of the last year at primary school, and the children always come back with stories and new friends.

The centre runs activities for school groups all year. The staff are trained instructors, and the centre has a good safety record. The children will try climbing, canoeing, a ropes course, orienteering in the woods and a night walk. Each evening there is a group activity, such as a quiz or a camp fire with songs.

The children sleep in rooms of six, with bunk beds. Girls and boys are on separate floors. Teachers sleep on each floor. Meals are in the dining hall, and the cooks can make food for any diet if they know in advance. Please tell us about allergies on the medical form.

The cost of the trip is 285 pounds per child, which includes the coach, the rooms, all meals and all activities. You can pay in three parts: 85 pounds by the end of January, 100 pounds by the end of March, and 100 pounds by the middle of May. If the cost is a problem for your family, please speak to Mrs Hale in confidence. The school has a fund to help, and no child will miss the trip because of money.

The children need warm clothes, a waterproof coat, old trainers that can get wet, wellington boots, a sleeping bag and a torch. A full kit list comes with this letter. Please put names on everything. Phones and games stay at home. The teachers will post photos on the school page each evening, so you can see what the children do.

Medicines must be in the original box with the name of the child. The teachers will give them at the correct times. If your child has asthma, please send two inhalers.

To book a place, please return the consent form and the medical form, with the first payment, by Friday 31 January. We have 58 places for the 60 children in Year 6, so we hope to take everyone. If we get more forms than places, we will make a waiting list in the order the forms arrive.

There is an information evening for parents on Thursday 6 February at six o'clock in the school hall. The head of the centre will show photos and answer questions. Children are welcome too.

Thank you for your support.

Mr Daniel Oyelaran, Year 6 teacher, Brookfield Primary School
