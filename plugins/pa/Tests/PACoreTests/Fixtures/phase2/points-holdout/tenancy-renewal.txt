Tenancy agreement, part 3: rent, deposit and renewal

Property: Flat 2, 41 Cobden Terrace
Landlord: Marlow Lettings on behalf of the owner
Tenant: Ines Carvalho

This part of the agreement sets out the rent, the deposit, the rules for a change of the rent, and the steps to renew or end the tenancy. Read it together with part 1, which lists the people and the property, and part 2, which lists the duties of each side.

3.1 The term. The tenancy started on 1 February 2026 for a fixed term of twelve months. At the end of the fixed term, the tenancy continues month to month on the same terms, unless one side gives notice or both sides sign a new fixed term.

3.2 The rent. The rent is paid monthly in advance on the first day of each month by standing order. The landlord gives a receipt on request. If a payment is more than 14 days late, the landlord may charge interest at the rate that the law allows, and no other fee.

3.3 The new rent. The landlord has reviewed the rent for the next term, in line with the rents of similar flats in the area.

From 1 February 2027, the monthly rent will be 1,180 pounds, which is an increase of 45 pounds on the current rent.

The landlord will not change the rent again for at least twelve months after that date. Any later change needs written notice of at least one month, as the law requires.

3.4 The deposit. The deposit of 1,250 pounds is held in a government-approved deposit scheme. The deposit stays the same for the new term. At the end of the tenancy, the landlord returns the deposit within ten days of the agreement of any deductions. Fair wear and tear is not a reason for a deduction.

3.5 Renewal. The landlord offers a new fixed term of twelve months at the new rent.

If you want to renew for a new fixed term, you must sign and return the renewal form that comes with this part of the agreement.

The landlord must receive the signed renewal form no later than 31 December 2026, so that the new term can start on 1 February 2027.

If the landlord does not receive the form by that date, the tenancy continues month to month at the new rent. In that case, either side can end it with notice, as set out in 3.6.

3.6 Notice to end. The tenant can end a month-to-month tenancy with one month of written notice, which ends on the last day of a rent period. The landlord must give the notice that the law requires, and must use the correct form. During a fixed term, neither side can end the tenancy early, except by agreement or for a reason that the law allows.

3.7 Inspections. The landlord may inspect the flat twice a year with 24 hours of written notice. The tenant may be present. The landlord will report any repairs that are needed and the date when they will be done.

3.8 Contact. Send all notices and forms to Marlow Lettings by email or by post. A notice by text message is not valid.
