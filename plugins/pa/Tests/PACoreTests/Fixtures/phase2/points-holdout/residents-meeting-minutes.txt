Minutes of the residents' meeting, Beacon Court

Held in the community room on Wednesday evening. Present: 23 residents from 19 flats, the chair Grace Lindqvist, the treasurer Omar Haddad, and Lucy Ford from the managing agent.

1. Welcome. Grace thanked everyone for coming. She said that the main topics were the state of the roof, the cost of the repair, and the plan for the garden. Apologies were received from five residents.

2. Minutes of the last meeting. The minutes of the June meeting were agreed as a correct record.

3. The roof. Lucy reported on the survey that the agent ordered after the leaks in flats 14 and 15. The surveyor found that the flat roof over the north block is at the end of its life. Patch repairs will not last more than one more winter. The surveyor recommended a full new roof covering with better insulation, which will also cut heating costs on the top floor.

Omar explained the money. The reserve fund holds about 41,000 pounds, which is not enough for the full work.

The meeting voted 21 to 2 to replace the roof covering of the north block in the spring, and to use the reserve fund for part of the cost.

The cost to each flat for the rest of the work is a one-off charge of 1,350 pounds, on top of the normal service charge.

Several residents asked if they could pay in parts. Lucy said that the agent can offer three equal parts, but the first part must be paid with the normal payment. Residents who have difficulty should speak to Lucy in private.

Each flat must pay the one-off charge, or the first of three parts, by 31 January 2027, so that the agent can book the roofers for March.

Lucy will send a letter to each flat with the exact amount and the bank details within two weeks.

4. The garden. Priyanka from flat 3 showed a plan for raised beds and a bench near the bins. The cost is small and the garden group will do most of the work. The meeting agreed the plan. The garden group will report at the next meeting.

5. Parking. Some residents said that visitors use the spaces of residents. The agent will put up new signs. No other action was agreed.

6. Any other business. A resident asked about the broken light on the second floor stairs. Lucy said that the repair was booked for Friday.

7. Next meeting. The next meeting will be in April, after the roof work starts. Grace closed the meeting and thanked Lucy for her report.
