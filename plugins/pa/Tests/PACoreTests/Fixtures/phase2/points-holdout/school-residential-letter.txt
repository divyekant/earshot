Oakfield Primary School

To the parents and carers of Year 6

Subject: the Year 6 residential trip to Hollow Pike Outdoor Centre

Dear Parents and Carers,

We are happy to tell you about the residential trip for Year 6 this year. Every year, our oldest pupils spend three days at an outdoor centre before they move on to secondary school. The trip builds confidence and friendship, and many former pupils tell us that it was the best part of their last year with us.

This year we will go to Hollow Pike Outdoor Centre, which is about two hours from the school by coach. The centre has qualified instructors for every activity, and it has worked with schools for more than twenty years. The children will sleep in rooms of four to six, with a member of our staff on each corridor.

The activities include climbing, canoeing on the lake, a night walk, team games, and a day of map reading in the forest. Each child can choose not to take part in an activity, and nobody will be pushed to do something that they find too hard. The instructors are good at helping nervous children to try a little at a time.

The trip will run from Wednesday 13 May to Friday 15 May 2026. The coach will leave the school at nine o'clock and will come back at about four o'clock on the Friday.

The cost of the trip for each child is 245 pounds, which covers the coach, the food, the rooms, the instructors and the insurance.

We know that this is a lot of money for some families. You can pay in three parts, and the school can help with part of the cost for families who need it. Please speak to Mrs Bell in the office in confidence. No child will miss the trip because of money.

To give your child a place, please complete the consent and medical form on the parent app and pay the first part of 85 pounds.

The consent form and the first payment must reach us by Friday 6 March 2026, because the centre needs our final numbers on that date.

The second and third parts of 80 pounds each are due at the start of April and at the start of May. We will send a reminder through the app before each date.

We will hold a meeting for parents in the school hall a few weeks before the trip. At the meeting, we will give you the kit list, the plan for each day, and the rules about phones and snacks. You can also meet the staff who will go on the trip and ask any questions.

If your child has a medical condition, a special diet or any worries about being away from home, please tell us on the form. We will speak to you before the trip to make a plan.

Thank you for your support.

Yours sincerely,

Mr Patel
Year 6 Leader
