Your boiler warranty

Boiler: Calderwood Combi 30, serial number on the data plate under the front cover
Installed by: Ashby Plumbing and Heating
Date of installation: 22 September 2026

Thank you for choosing a Calderwood boiler. This document explains your warranty: what it covers, how long it lasts, and the conditions that you must meet to keep it valid. Please keep it with the installation certificate and the user guide.

Your boiler comes with a standard warranty of two years from the date of installation. Because your installer is a Calderwood approved installer, you can extend the warranty at no extra cost.

To get the extended warranty of ten years, you must register the boiler on the Calderwood website or by phone.

Registration must be done within 60 days of the date of installation, which for your boiler is by 21 November 2026.

If you do not register within this time, the warranty stays at two years. We cannot extend it later, even if the boiler is serviced every year.

The warranty covers the repair or the replacement of any part of the boiler that fails because of a fault in the materials or the making of the boiler. It covers the parts and the labour of our own engineers. It does not cover faults that are caused by the water system, for example sludge, scale or frozen pipes, or by work on the boiler by someone who is not qualified.

To keep the warranty valid, the boiler must be serviced once every year by a qualified engineer, and the service must be recorded in the service record at the back of the user guide.

The first service is due within twelve months of the date of installation. Keep the receipt of each service. If you need to make a claim, we may ask to see the service record and the receipts. A missed service can make the warranty invalid for faults that a service would have found.

A service by a Calderwood engineer costs 95 pounds, including the parts that a normal service needs, such as a new seal for the burner. You can also use any other qualified engineer.

If your boiler stops working, first check the simple things in the user guide: the gas supply, the power, and the pressure on the gauge, which should be between 1 and 1.5 bar when the boiler is cold. If the problem continues, call our service line. We aim to visit within two working days, and within one working day if you have no heating in winter and someone in the home is over 75, under 5, or unwell.

If you move house, the warranty passes to the new owner of the boiler. Tell us the name of the new owner, so that we can update our records.

Your rights under consumer law are not affected by this warranty.
