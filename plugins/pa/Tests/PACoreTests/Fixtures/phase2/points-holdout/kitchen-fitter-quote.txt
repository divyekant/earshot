Dear Ms Okafor,

Thank you for showing us your kitchen last Thursday. It was good to meet you and to see the units that you ordered from the supplier. This letter gives our quote for the fitting work, what it includes, and what we need from you before we can book the dates.

We looked at the room with care. The walls are mostly straight, but the wall behind the sink leans out by about 12 mm at the top. We will scribe the end panel to fit, so the gap will not show. The floor is level enough for the base units, and we do not need to add a new floor layer.

The work includes the removal of the old units and worktops, the fitting of 14 base and wall units, the cutting and fitting of the oak worktops, the fitting of the sink and the hob into the worktop, and the fitting of the plinths, the end panels and the cornice. We will also fit the handles that you chose. We will take the old units away to the recycling centre.

The work does not include plumbing, electrics, tiling or decoration. Your plumber must connect the sink and the dishwasher after we finish the worktops. Your electrician must connect the hob and the oven. We can give you the names of people that we work with, if you want them.

The total price for the fitting is 3,450 pounds, including VAT and the removal of the old units.

We estimate that the work will take six working days. The first two days are the removal and the base units, the third day is the wall units, and the last three days are the worktops, the panels and the finishing work. The worktops need one day to settle in the room before we cut them, so please let us deliver them the week before.

Our next free dates start on Monday 9 March 2026. We can hold these dates for you for a short time only, because other customers are waiting for the spring.

To confirm the booking, please sign the attached form and pay the deposit of 500 pounds to the bank account on the form.

We must have your signed form and the deposit by Friday 20 February 2026, or we will give the dates to the next customer on our list.

The rest of the price is due on the last day of the work, after you have checked the kitchen with us. If you find a fault later, we will come back to fix it at no cost for twelve months after the end of the work.

Please keep the room clear on the first morning, and make sure that the water to the kitchen is turned off. We start at eight o'clock and finish at about four o'clock each day. We use dust sheets and we vacuum at the end of each day.

If you have any questions about this quote, please call Dan on the number at the top of this letter.
