Changes to your arranged overdraft

Dear Mr Osei,

We are writing to tell you about a change to the arranged overdraft on your current account. We review overdrafts each year. We look at how each customer uses the overdraft and at the information that credit reference agencies give us. This letter explains what will change, why, and what you can do.

At the moment, your arranged overdraft limit is 2,000 pounds. Over the last twelve months, your account was overdrawn on most days, and the balance stayed close to the limit for long periods. We want to help you to reduce your use of the overdraft, because it is an expensive way to borrow over a long time.

We have decided to reduce your arranged overdraft limit from 2,000 pounds to 1,200 pounds.

The new limit will start on 15 January 2027.

If your balance is below minus 1,200 pounds on that date, the part of the balance above the new limit will become an unarranged overdraft, and we may refuse payments from your account.

The interest rate on your arranged overdraft does not change. It stays at 39.9 percent per year. There is no fee for an unarranged overdraft, but we will not let payments take your balance below the new limit, and we may return direct debits and standing orders unpaid.

If you want to keep a higher limit, or if you are worried about the change, please call us before the new limit starts so that we can talk about your situation.

We can look at your income and your costs with you and agree a plan. A plan might be a lower limit that goes down by small steps, or a loan at a lower rate to pay off the overdraft over a fixed time. We will not charge for this conversation, and it will not appear on your credit file.

There are also free and independent services that can help you with money worries. Their details are on the back of this letter. They can help you to make a budget and to talk to other lenders, if you have other debts.

You can check your balance and your overdraft at any time in the mobile app. You can also set an alert in the app that tells you when your balance falls below an amount that you choose.

You do not need to reply to this letter if you agree with the change. The new limit will start on the date above.

Yours sincerely,
