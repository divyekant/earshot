Your claim for water damage, reference CL-20931

Dear Mr and Mrs Lindgren,

Thank you for the information and the photos that you sent about the leak from the washing machine. We are sorry about the damage to your home. This letter tells you our decision on your claim, the amount that we will pay, and what you need to do next.

Our loss adjuster visited your home and wrote a report. The report confirms that the hose of the washing machine split, and that water ran under the kitchen floor and into the hall. The adjuster found damage to the laminate floor of the kitchen and the hall, the bottom of two kitchen units, and the plaster at the base of the hall wall. The washing machine itself is not covered, because the policy does not cover the part that failed.

We have accepted your claim for the damage to the floors, the kitchen units and the plaster.

The adjuster priced the repair work with the costs of local trades. The total cost of the work is 4,920 pounds. Your policy has an excess of 350 pounds for escape of water claims, which we take off the total.

We will pay you 4,570 pounds, which is the cost of the repair work less the excess of 350 pounds.

You can use your own contractors, or you can use the contractors on our panel, who give a guarantee of twelve months on their work. If you use your own contractors and their price is higher than the adjuster's price, we will only pay the adjuster's price unless you agree the difference with us first.

The floor must be completely dry before new laminate goes down. The adjuster left a drying machine in the hall. A technician will come back to read the moisture levels and to collect the machine when the floor is dry. This normally takes two to three weeks.

To receive the payment, you must sign the settlement form and return it to us with your bank details.

Please return the signed form within 30 days of the date of this letter, which is by 12 December 2026, or we will need to review the claim again.

When we receive the form, we will pay the money into your account within five working days.

Please keep the receipts for any temporary costs, for example the cost of a launderette while your kitchen is not usable. Some of these costs may be covered under the section for extra costs of your policy.

This claim will not change the price of your policy at your next renewal by more than the normal limits in your policy terms. If you have any questions about this decision, call our claims team and give the reference number at the top of this letter.

Yours sincerely,

Claims Team
