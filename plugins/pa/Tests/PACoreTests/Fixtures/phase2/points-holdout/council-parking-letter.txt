Your reference: resident permit application RP-88214

Dear Resident,

Thank you for your application for a resident parking permit for Zone C. We have checked your application and the documents that you sent. This letter tells you the result, what you must do next, and the dates that apply to you.

Zone C covers the streets around the station, including Larch Road, Fern Street and the north end of Quarry Hill. Parking in Zone C is for permit holders only from 8 am to 6:30 pm, Monday to Saturday. Outside these hours, anyone can park in the bays. The zone does not include the car park of the station, which has its own charges.

We can approve your application, but one document is missing. You sent a copy of your vehicle registration document and a council tax bill. These show that you live in the zone and that the vehicle is yours. However, the registration document still shows your old address.

Before we can issue the permit, you must send us proof that the address on your vehicle registration document has been changed to your address in Zone C.

You can send a photo of the new registration document, or the letter from the vehicle licensing agency that confirms the change. Please send it through the online portal, with your reference number, or by post to the address at the top of this letter.

The cost of a resident permit for one year is 96 pounds for your vehicle, which is in the middle band for emissions.

If you have a second vehicle at the same address, a permit for it costs 140 pounds. Visitor permits are sold in books of 20 for 30 pounds.

We will hold your application open until 15 December 2026, and if we do not receive the proof by then, we will close the application and you will need to apply again.

While your application is open, you may park in Zone C with the temporary permit that we sent with this letter. Put it on the dashboard where a warden can read it. The temporary permit is valid for four weeks from the date of this letter. If you park without a valid permit, a warden may give you a penalty charge notice.

When we receive the proof, we will take the payment from the card that you gave us in your application. We will send the permit by post within ten working days. Your permit will be digital as well, so a warden can check it from the registration of your vehicle.

If your circumstances change, for example if you sell the car or move out of the zone, you must tell us within 28 days. We may refund part of the cost for the full months that are left.

Yours faithfully,

Parking Services Team
